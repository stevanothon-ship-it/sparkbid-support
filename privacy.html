Privacy Policy

SparkBid — bids, change orders, and estimates for electrical contractors

Last updated: July 27, 2026

What we collect

SparkBid is a business tool. Nearly everything it stores is data you enter yourself to run your business:

  • Account info: the email address and password you sign up with (handled by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your password directly).
  • Business data you enter: your company profile, customer contacts (name, phone, email, address), jobs, estimates, change orders, rate list items, photos you attach to change orders, and signatures captured on estimates or change orders.
  • AI Ballpark Estimate inputs: when you use the AI Ballpark Estimate feature, the job description text you type is sent to Google's AI models (via Firebase AI Logic) to generate a pricing response. No other app data is sent along with it.

How we use it

Your data is used solely to operate the app for you: syncing your jobs and estimates across your own devices, generating PDFs you choose to share, and powering the optional AI Ballpark Estimate feature. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored on Google's Firebase platform (Firestore database and Cloud Storage), under industry-standard security practices. Only you can access your own data — each account's data is isolated and requires your authentication to read or write.

Subscriptions & payment

The AI Ballpark Estimate subscription is billed and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. SparkBid never receives or stores your payment card details. Manage or cancel your subscription any time in your device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your customers, jobs, and rate list at any time within the app. To delete your account and all associated data entirely, email us at the address below and we'll take care of it promptly.

Children

SparkBid is a business tool for electrical contractors and is not directed at, or knowingly used to collect information from, children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of SparkBid after a change means you accept the updated policy.
